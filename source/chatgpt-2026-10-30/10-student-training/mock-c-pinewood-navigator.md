# Mock C | Pinewood Housing Resource Navigator

Advanced student exercise | 300-minute local target | Not an official SkillsUSA prompt

## Client

The fictional Pinewood Housing Resource Navigator helps visitors on shared or low-bandwidth computers understand available informational services. The site must never request private financial or identity data. Primary user task: find a service type, read its schedule and locate a contact method.

## Client-approved facts

- General office: Monday–Thursday, 9:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.
- General contact: help@pinewood.example.

| Service | Type | Schedule | Email |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rental Guidance | Information | Weekdays 9:00 a.m.–noon | rental@pinewood.example |
| Budget Coaching | Education | Tuesday 1:00–3:00 p.m. | budget@pinewood.example |
| Accessibility Resources | Assistance | Wednesday 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. | access@pinewood.example |
| Documentation Help | Assistance | Thursday 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. | docs@pinewood.example |

## Acceptance criteria

1. Write five discovery questions and three testable requirements before coding.
2. Build clear, responsive navigation and four accurate service cards.
3. Add an accessible filter by resource type with count and empty state.
4. State that the page is informational and users must not submit private details.
5. Verify all page behavior at 320px and with keyboard alone.
6. Record eight tests, a defect report and a known limitation.
7. Explain scope: what the site provides, what it cannot verify and what should remain outside the project.

## Evidence and review

Submit a small-screen sketch, source code, client notes, testing matrix and two-minute demonstration. The instructor can release two separate change requests after code freeze. Record each change and retest previous working behavior.

## Coaching boundary

No service is real; .example email addresses are fictional. This is a locally authored exercise, not an official competition task.
