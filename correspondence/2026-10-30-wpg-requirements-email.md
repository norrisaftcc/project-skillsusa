# Email to Web Professionals Global: requirements and logistics

- **Status:** draft, 30 October 2026. Not sent from this repo; update this line when it is sent.
- **To:** membership@WebProfessionalsGlobal.org (the classroom-resources contact named in the [Web Pro Contests FAQ](https://webprocontests.org/faq))
- **Purpose:** ask for requirements and logistics only. We build our own training materials from published requirements, so this email does not ask for training content.

**Subject:** New postsecondary team for Web Design and Development: requirements and logistics

Hello,

Fayetteville Technical Community College (Fayetteville, NC) has an active SkillsUSA chapter, but this is our first year in the Web Design and Development contest. We plan to enter a postsecondary team at the North Carolina State Leadership and Skills Conference in April 2027. We are building our own training plan and want to align it with your requirements. Could you help with the questions below?

1. Rubric and topic guideline. Your FAQ says WPG publishes the contest rubric each year and that the technical committee publishes a topic guideline, both available through SkillsUSA. Through which SkillsUSA channel or contact should a new advisor get the current versions?

2. Certification exams for students. We would like our students to sit the Certified Web Designer Novice exam in late January and the Apprentice exam in March.
   - How do we register students, and what does each exam cost?
   - What is the passing score?
   - How are the exams proctored, and can we test on our campus?
   - How do the Novice and Apprentice levels differ?

3. Contest environment. What does the browser-based coding environment allow? For example, can both team members edit at the same time? Is there a way for students to try the environment before the contest?

4. State contests. Does SkillsUSA North Carolina use the WPG state competition package for Web Design? If not, who should we contact about how the NC contest is run?

5. Educators. Is there a membership or registration we should complete as the team's advisor?

Thank you for your help. We look forward to competing.
