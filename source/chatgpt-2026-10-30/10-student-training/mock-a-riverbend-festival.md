# Mock A | Riverbend Neighborhood Festival

Student exercise | 180-minute local target | Not an official SkillsUSA prompt

## Client

Residents need to choose activities at the fictional Riverbend Neighborhood Festival on Saturday, May 15, 2027. All event information is classroom-only and fictional. Primary task: find an activity by category and see its start time and location.

## Client-approved facts

- Event location: Riverbend Park, 100 Park Lane, Riverbend.
- Information: info@riverbend.example.

| Activity | Category | Start | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pondside Yoga | Wellness | 9:00 a.m. | Meadow Lawn |
| Neighborhood Makers | Arts | 10:30 a.m. | Craft Hall |
| Code Discovery Lab | Learning | 11:15 a.m. | Innovation Tent |
| Community Concert | Arts | 2:00 p.m. | Main Stage |

## Acceptance criteria

1. Clear page title, event date and location, skip link, landmarks and navigation.
2. Four activity cards, with exact activity names, category, times and places.
3. Accessible native select filter with All, Wellness, Arts, Learning.
4. Accurate and announced result count; selecting All restores all four.
5. No overflow at 320px, 768px or desktop; readable content, visible keyboard focus.
6. Contact email link and no broken local files or console errors.
7. Written client questions, one mobile wireframe and EVIDENCE.md with pass/fail tests and one repaired defect.

## Submission

Use index.html, styles.css, app.js and relative local assets. Explain how your layout helps a visitor find a suitable event. Instructor may release a separate timed change request after the initial code freeze. Do not assume the initial facts will remain unchanged.
