## Client brief

The fictional Community Resource Desk helps residents find three kinds of services. The client needs a clear, usable, responsive website. A person must be able to find a service and send an email without help.

## Client facts

Use these facts exactly. All names and addresses are fictional; `.example` addresses are not real inboxes.

General office hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.

| Service | Category | Hours | Email |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food Support | Daily Needs | Weekdays, 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. | food@resource.example |
| Career Coaching | Career | Tuesday and Thursday, 1:00–4:00 p.m. | career@resource.example |
| Digital Help Desk | Technology | Wednesday, 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. | tech@resource.example |

General questions: hello@resource.example

## Required features

1. A page title, a clear main heading, and working navigation to the Services and Contact sections.
2. All three services with their exact names, categories, hours and email links.
3. A category filter with All services, Daily Needs, Career and Technology. Choosing All services shows all three again.
4. A mobile-friendly layout that works at 320 px with no sideways scrolling.
5. Semantic HTML, labels, visible keyboard focus, and full use without a mouse.
6. No missing files and no JavaScript errors in the browser console.
7. An `EVIDENCE.md` file (see [the evidence file](../#the-evidence-file)).

## Before you hand in

Check the phone layout, keyboard Tab order, the category filter, every email link, every fact, and the browser console. Hand in your files and `EVIDENCE.md`. Be ready to explain what you built and why.

**As a team of two:** one partner leads the layout and content, the other leads the filter. Both explain the whole page. See [practice as a team](../#practice-as-a-team-of-two).
