# PRACTICE ONLY — NOT AN OFFICIAL SKILLSUSA COMPETITION BRIEF

North Carolina Community Repair Café

Suggested duration: four hours. Build from blank files with a partner.

## CLIENT

A fictional community group holds free repair events for household items. Visitors need to understand what can be repaired, when to visit and how to register interest.

## DELIVERABLE

Build a responsive website with a home view, event information and an accessible registration-interest form. You may use separate pages or clear sections. Do not collect real personal information; demonstrate form validation and clearly label the submission as a practice demo.

## ACCEPTANCE CHECKS

- A visitor can find the next event, accepted items and a clear call to action. Invented event details must be labeled as fictional.
- Navigation and core tasks work with a keyboard and on a narrow screen.
- Form fields have labels and understandable validation feedback.
- The site uses consistent type, color, spacing and optimized assets.
- The team provides a wireframe, asset-source log, decision notes and a short test log.
- Both students can explain their decisions and show their own code.

## OPTIONAL CLIENT CHANGE

After two hours, announce a venue change and add a prominent notice without breaking the main user journey.

## REVIEW

Use the six visible categories in the supplied August 29, 2026 sample rubric. Choose one concrete improvement for the next practice. The mock schedule, content and change request are recommendations only.
