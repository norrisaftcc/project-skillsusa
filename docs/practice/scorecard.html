This is not the official rubric

These weights are our own Local practice rule. The official rubric is member material; coaches get it through SkillsUSA. Use this card to compare one practice build with the next, not to predict a contest score.

How to score

  • Score what you can see: a working feature, a passed test, a clear explanation. Do not score taste.
  • Write one reason for each score in the evidence notes.
  • Before a mock contest, two coaches score the same sample build and settle large differences.
  • After scoring, pick the two weakest areas, practice them in a skill lab, and retest the same behavior.
