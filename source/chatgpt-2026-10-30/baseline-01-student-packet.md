## Client brief: Community Resource Desk

The fictional Community Resource Desk helps residents locate three kinds of services. Your client needs a clear, usable, responsive website. A person should be able to identify a service and send an email without asking for help.

## Required client facts

General office hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.

| Service | Category | Hours | Email |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food Support | Daily Needs | Weekdays, 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. | food@resource.example |
| Career Coaching | Career | Tuesday and Thursday, 1:00–4:00 p.m. | career@resource.example |
| Digital Help Desk | Technology | Wednesday, 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. | tech@resource.example |

General questions: hello@resource.example

All names and email addresses are fictional; .example addresses are not real service inboxes.

## Required features

1. Page title, clear main heading, working navigation to Services and Contact sections.
2. All three services with exact names, categories, hours, and email links.
3. Category filter with All services, Daily Needs, Career, and Technology. Filter must restore all services.
4. Mobile-friendly layout, including at 320px, without sideways scrolling.
5. Semantic HTML, labels, visible keyboard focus, and use without a mouse.
6. No missing resources or browser console JavaScript errors.
7. A short NOTES.md with audience, two design choices, three verification checks, known issues, and next steps.

## Local practice schedule

Plan for 5–10 minutes. Spend approximately 65–70 minutes implementing. Reserve 15 minutes to test and explain. Total target: 90 minutes. These are training timeboxes, NOT official SkillsUSA limits.

Use the supplied index.html, styles.css, app.js. No AI code generation or full-page templates during this local independent baseline. Official rules must be verified separately.

## Optional 20-minute change request

A Saturday emergency referral line is available from 10:00 a.m. to noon. Add an obvious, accessible announcement near the top. Retain existing weekday office hours.

## Student review

Check phone layout, keyboard Tab navigation, category filter, service links, factual accuracy, and browser console. Submit source files and NOTES.md. Be ready to explain what you built and why.
