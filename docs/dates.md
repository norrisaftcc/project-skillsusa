## Path to nationals

1. **Regional rally** (optional). Practice, and a lower state registration fee.
2. **NC State Leadership and Skills Conference**, 21–23 April 2027, Greensboro.
3. **First place** in a national contest at state qualifies for nationals. If the winner is not eligible (for example, joined after 1 March), NC moves the place to the next finisher.
4. **National Leadership and Skills Conference (NLSC)**, 21–25 June 2027, Atlanta.

Status: {% include status.html s="confirmed" %} ([SkillsUSA NC](https://www.skillsusanc.org/skillsusa-membership-deadline)).

We found no rule that makes the regional rally a requirement for college/postsecondary competitors. {% include status.html s="unverified" %}

## Membership and dues

Every competitor must be a paid member by **1 March 2027**. {% include status.html s="confirmed" %}

| Member type | National | NC state | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student (college/postsecondary) | $8 | $6 | $14 |
| Professional (advisor) | $20 | $6 | $26 |

Source: [SkillsUSA NC join page](https://skillsusanc.org/join). {% include status.html s="confirmed" %} A college can also join through the NC Campus Affiliation Plan instead of individual dues.

## Event fees

These are the **2026** amounts. The 2027 amounts will be in the 2027 packet. {% include status.html s="unverified" %}

| Fee | 2026 amount |
|---|---|
| Regional rally, per person (student or advisor) | $20 |
| State registration, after a regional rally | $60 |
| State registration, without a regional rally | $85 |
| Late addition | $25 each |
| Change after the drop deadline | $25, or $50 on site |
| Card payment surcharge | 4% |

Sources: [regional rallies](https://www.skillsusanc.org/regional-rallies), [state conference information](https://www.skillsusanc.org/state-conference-information). The school must pay its balance before its students can compete.
