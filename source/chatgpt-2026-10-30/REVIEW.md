# Review: ChatGPT material, 30 October 2026

Reviewed 6 November 2026 by Claude. This review covers the nine documents in this folder (see [MANIFEST.md](MANIFEST.md)).

Labels:

- **WRONG**: a public source contradicts the statement.
- **UNVERIFIED**: no public source confirms the statement. Do not publish it as fact.
- **CONFLICT**: two documents in this set disagree.
- **GAP**: the material is referenced but missing, or the contest needs it and the material does not supply it.

## 1. Summary

The material is sound practice content. The three mock briefs, the baseline, the ten labs and the local rubric agree with each other. Their fictional facts are consistent with the change cards, and the dates are correct (15 May 2027 is a Saturday). The material marks its own times and scores as local, not official, and it does this consistently.

There are four problems:

1. The practice format is for one student. The contest is for a team of two.
2. The written test practice (40 questions, 45 minutes) does not match the probable contest format (70 questions, 60 minutes).
3. The Claude addendum of 30 October has one factual error: the 2026 postsecondary contest hours (section 2). (Corrected 6 November. This line first said two errors. The resume penalty is not an error.)
4. Most of the working files (labs, starters, answer keys, the practice hub) are in ZIP packs that are not in the Drive folder.

## 3. Conflicts inside the material

1. **CONFLICT: one student or a team.** The baseline packet, the coach guide, the three mocks and the runbook speak to one student. The contest has a team of two, and teamwork is one of the three judging areas. The Training Track addendum says so, but the exercises do not change. Each mock needs a team version: roles, a handoff point, and a team interview while the team codes.
2. **CONFLICT: written test format.** The Field Manual and the 12-Week Plan (week 10) use 40 questions in 45 minutes. The addendum says 70 questions in 60 minutes. Practise at 70 in 60. Confirm the contest format in the 2026–28 standard.
3. **CONFLICT: local files or a browser IDE.** The baseline packet, the coach guide ("Open the page from local files") and Mock A ("relative local assets") assume local files. The contest uses a cloud IDE. Local files are acceptable for early labs. For mocks, practise in a browser-based editor.
4. **CONFLICT: the evidence file name.** The baseline asks for `NOTES.md`. The labs and the mocks ask for `EVIDENCE.md`. Use one name.
5. **CONFLICT: the baseline contact method.** The Training Track allows "an accessible contact link or form stub". The student packet asks only for email links. The coach guide checks a "form label". The only required form control is the category filter. Change the coach guide to "filter label", or add a form to the brief.
6. **CONFLICT: baseline timing.** The Training Track gives 75 minutes to build and 15 to test (90 minutes). The student packet gives 5–10 to plan, 65–70 to build and 15 to test (85–95 minutes). The difference is small. Use the student packet figures.
7. **CONFLICT: rubric names.** The four copies of the local rubric use the same weights (25/20/20/15/10/10) but different names for each area. Use one set of names. Map them to the three national judging areas.
8. **CONFLICT: the baseline filter.** The Training Track makes the filter a stretch goal. The student packet makes it required feature 3. The student packet is newer (modified 4 November).
9. **Possible data problem, Mock C.** Rental Guidance is open "Weekdays", but the office is open Monday–Thursday. This can be a deliberate test for a discovery question. If so, say so in the coach notes. If not, change "Weekdays" to "Monday–Thursday".

## 4. Gaps

1. **GAP: ZIP packs.** The ten full lab prompts, the starter files, the two reference sites, the knowledge check and answer key, the CSV templates, the interview bank, the remediation map and the offline hub are not in the Drive folder. They are in the ChatGPT project conversation. Export them before that conversation is lost.
2. **GAP: paper wireframe hour.** The contest gives one hour for paper wireframes before coding. LAB-01 gives 25 minutes, and the mocks ask for "one mobile wireframe" with no time box. Add a timed paper-wireframe step to each mock.
3. **GAP: live client interview.** The mocks give the client facts in writing. The contest starts with a live client interview. A coach must play the client, and the brief must hold back some facts until the team asks.
4. **GAP: calendar.** The 12-week plan has no dates. The NC state conference date is not published. Fix the calendar when NC publishes the 2027 packet.
5. **GAP: Computer Programming.** The Training Track proposes a contest selector for Web Development and Computer Programming. This material covers only Web Development.
6. **GAP: the October 9 research document and the Baseline report.** Both are cited but are not in this folder. They are expected with the next research import.

## 5. Effect on the Pages site

1. **Do not publish Technical Standards text.** SkillsUSA does not allow it in a public location. Link to Pathful instead.
2. **Do not publish the change cards, the answer key or the reference sites.** The runbook marks the change cards "instructor only". The Field Manual says surprise changes must stay outside the student work area. This repo is public. **Decision, 6 November 2026:** instructor-only material stays in Google Drive and is not committed to this repo. A private repo may hold it later. For this reason the runbook is not imported (see MANIFEST.md).
3. **Publish the student material.** The field manual, the lab catalog, the baseline packet and the three mock briefs are original, fictional and safe to publish.
4. **Label every rule.** Each rule statement on the site gets a status: confirmed (with source and check date), unverified, or local practice rule.
